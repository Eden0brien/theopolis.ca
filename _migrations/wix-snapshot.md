## ABOUT US — `/about-us`

**H1:** About Us

**Mission:**
> "Theopolis is equipping a rising generation of Catholic artists and creating a space of encounter between the Secular and Sacred"

**Our Story**
Theopolis originated from conversations among friends interested in art, culture, community, and Catholic tradition. The organization evolved from study and reflection across Ontario, the U.S.A., U.K., Italy, and Greece into a mission to revive Catholic art within communities. It draws inspiration from Acts 8:26–40, the story of Apostle Philip and the Ethiopian Eunuch. This passage guides their mission to empower Catholic artists to create initial encounters between the sacred and "the 'unwitting world'" so audiences might be "poised by a beauty that it sees and hears without understanding" and ask "how can I [understand] unless someone instructs me?" Through this vision, Theopolis aims to share Catholic artistic tradition globally, "stirring hearts and lifting the gaze heaven-ward."

**What is Catholic Art?**
Catholic art encompasses creative expressions—painting, sculpture, music, literature, film, photography—created within the spirit of the Faith. The organization prizes creative excellence and welcomes diverse themes, styles, and subjects. Valued works:
- Are original and imaginative
- Offer new perspective
- Contemplate the transcendentals
- Deal with substance and content
- Engage the complexity of sin and suffering
- Celebrate the dignity of mankind
- Much more!

> "Catholic art, in our reckoning, engages the world through imagination, beauty, and truth."

**Meet the Team**

*Eden O'Brien — President and Co-Founder.* Holds an MLitt in Theological Aesthetics from the University of St Andrews and a B.A. in Philosophy from Our Lady Seat of Wisdom College. Her work explores the intersection of art and truth, with research in 19th- & 20th-century Aestheticism, Religion & Literature, and Tolkien's poetics. She co-edited a volume for the Edinburgh Journal of Inkling Studies and serves as associate editor for Transpositions. She has spoken at numerous events and currently works in Business Development across North America and Europe.

*Monica Sifert — Co-Founder.* Studied sacred art, fine art, and iconography in Florence, Italy; Crete, Greece; and Phoenix, Arizona. Holds a B.A. in Philosophy and Literature from Our Lady Seat of Wisdom College. Inspired by the Italian Masters, the Pre-Raphaelites, and the Arts & Crafts Movement. Passionate about craftsmanship; works as a Corporate Paralegal. Iconography and wood burning artwork at http://www.iconicstudioart.com/

## 2026 EXHIBITION — `/2026-exhibition`

**H1:** 2026 Exhibition

**Epigraph:**
> "Before me were no things created except eternal ones, and I endure eternal." — Dante, The Inferno, Canto III

The Soul has long been depicted in art and literature as the protagonist of a drama that stretches beyond life, death, and time.

In the introduction to her 1949 translation of The Divine Comedy, Dorothy L. Sayers writes that Dante's masterwork is "precisely the drama of the soul's choice." It is not a fairy story, but a great Christian allegory, deriving its power from the terror and splendour of the Christian revelation. Creative explorations such as Dante's reveal that this drama of eternal consequence lies at the heart of life's greatest questions and bears upon enduring concerns of good and evil, love, suffering, and faith. Yet, in an age marked by materialism, consumerism, and omnipresent technology, the reality of the Soul is easy to overlook, or to forget entirely.

**The Drama of the Soul** is a group exhibition organized by Theopolis, a collective of Catholic artists. The exhibition invites works that reflect on the existence, nature, and problems of the Soul. While informed by the Catholic intellectual and artistic tradition, submissions do not need to be explicitly religious. Artists are invited to engage the theme with seriousness and depth, remaining attentive to the gravity of the subject matter.
