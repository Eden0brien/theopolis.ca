# Image credits & rights

Every image on the site is recorded here: where it came from, what it shows, and its
licensing status. **Rule: never publish an image whose rights are unclear.**

All files were downloaded full-resolution from the old Wix CDN
(`static.wixstatic.com/media/...`) on 2026-06-18. "Original Wix id" is kept for traceability.

## ⚠ Rights to verify BEFORE publishing
- **`artists/baptism-fresco.jpg`** — original filename was `istockphoto-2221647269`, i.e. an
  **iStock** photo. The fresco itself (a Renaissance baptism scene, Masaccio-school) is public
  domain, but *this photograph of it* may be a licensed/watermarked stock image. Confirm a
  valid licence or replace with a public-domain photo before going live.
