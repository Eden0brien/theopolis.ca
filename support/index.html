Help Renew Faith & Culture Through Beauty

The renewal of culture has never been the work of artists alone. Throughout history, Catholic art has flourished through the support of patrons who understood the evangelical power of beauty and chose to invest in it.

Masaccio, The Tribute Money, Brancacci Chapel (public domain).

Why Your Support Matters

Theopolis exists to restore Catholic art to the public square in Ontario by equipping artists to create work that awakens wonder, invites contemplation, and renews faith. Your support makes this work possible.
