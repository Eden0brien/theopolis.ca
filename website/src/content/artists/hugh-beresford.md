A Catholic singer-songwriter based in Ontario, Canada, with a passion for music, art,
and writing. Introduced to the liberal arts in his late teenage years, Hugh was drawn
to sharing faith through the good, the true, and the beautiful. He has taught, performed,
and composed music shaped by these values — work that seeks to reveal God's goodness
rather than to preach explicit religious themes.
