_Last updated: 18 June 2026_

Theopolis is committed to making this website accessible to as many people as possible,
including those who use assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard
navigation, or screen magnification.

## What we do

We aim to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**. In
building this site we have:

- Used clear, semantic HTML so structure is conveyed to assistive technology.
- Provided descriptive alternative text for meaningful images, and credited every artwork.
- Ensured the site can be navigated by keyboard, with a visible focus indicator and a
  "skip to content" link.
- Chosen colour combinations that meet AA contrast for body text.
- Respected the "reduce motion" setting for animations.
- Made the layout responsive, so it adapts to phones, tablets, and desktops.

## Known limitations

Some artwork images are reproductions of paintings and sculpture; their alternative text
describes the title and artist rather than the full visual content. We continue to improve
these descriptions.

## Feedback

If you encounter any barrier using this site, or need information in a different format,
please tell us through the contact form. We treat accessibility feedback as a priority and
will work with you to provide what you need.
